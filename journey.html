Our Journey

Advaya Educational and Charitable Trust began its journey in 2020 with a simple but powerful vision â€” to support children from migrant and marginalized communities who were denied access to basic education, nutrition, and care.

Celebrating 5 Years of Making a Difference

We proudly celebrate five meaningful years of service, learning, and transformation at Advaya Educational and Charitable Trust, since our humble beginning in 2020. What started with just four children under a tarpaulin sheet has now grown into a nurturing space for over 538 children from migrant and underprivileged backgrounds.

This journey has been full of challenges, growth, and countless small victories â€” made possible by the collective spirit of compassion and commitment.

Our mission has always been clear: to restore childhoods and build futures for children who are often left out of the mainstream due to poverty, migration, or social barriers. Over the years, we've provided quality education, nutrition, and a safe environment to children living in makeshift homes, helping them take their first steps towards a life of dignity and opportunity.

This milestone is not ours alone. It is because of generous supporters like you that we've been able to transform adversity into hope. Your faith in us has helped us bridge learning gaps, fight malnutrition, and bring joy to the lives of those who had no access to basic rights.

Key Milestones

Moments that defined our journey.

2020
The Beginning
  • Started with just 4 children, learning under a tarpaulin sheet
  • Focused on basic education and daily meals
  • Worked closely with migrant families living in makeshift settlements in Bengaluru
2021
Building Trust & Continuity
  • Continued education and nutrition support during COVID-19 pandemic
  • Introduced health check-ups, hygiene awareness, and relief support
  • Strengthened community relationships despite severe challenges
2022
Expanding Impact
  • Formalized Education Support Program
  • Introduced Bridge School & Day Care programs
  • Began enrolling children into formal schools for the first time
  • Nutrition program scaled to ensure no child studied hungry
2023
Reaching the Unreached
  • Launched the Mobile School Initiative to reach children in slums
  • Expanded Evening Study Centre for school-going children
  • Conducted multiple health camps, dental & eye check-ups
2024
Strengthening Outcomes
  • Supported 538+ children cumulatively
  • Distributed 6,000+ education kits
  • Empowered 320+ girls through education and care kits
  • Helped 27% children move out of malnutrition
  • Strengthened partnerships with NGOs, corporates, and donors
2025
Looking Ahead
  • Supporting 121 children daily with education and nutrition
  • Scaling programs to new communities
  • Strengthening systems, transparency, and team capacity
  • Continuing the mission to restore childhoods
