Health & Well-being

In the communities we serve, children often face daily challenges due to inadequate access to basic health care, hygiene awareness, and sanitation facilities. Poor living conditions and limited health education contribute to frequent illness, school absenteeism, and, in some cases, early dropout.

Recognizing the importance of health in education, we've integrated hygiene and WASH (Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene) facilities in schools, along with menstrual health support for adolescent girls. Regular BMI tracking, menstrual hygiene awareness, dental and eye camps help children grow in a safe and supportive environment.

These efforts have led to improved health outcomes, reduced absenteeism, and greater confidence among students—ensuring every child is not only educated but also healthy, protected, and empowered.

In addition to the camps, our teachers consistently monitored and recorded each child’s height and weight to track their physical development. When a medical issue was identified, our team ensured follow-up care, including arranging hospital visits, surgeries, and treatments in coordination with medical professionals and supporting organizations.

This consistent focus on healthcare has led to tangible improvements – better overall health, early identification of health concerns, and a noticeable reduction in illness-related absenteeism.

"For many children, this was the first time they were seen by a doctor or received any form of medical attention."
