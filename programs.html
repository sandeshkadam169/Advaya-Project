Our Programs

We implement comprehensive support programs designed to empower children through education, nutrition, and healthcare.

Day Care Programme

Nurturing environment for children aged 3-6 years, providing early childhood education and freeing older siblings to attend school.

Nutrition Support

Providing daily nutritious meals to tackle malnutrition and ensure healthy physical development for children in slums.

Healthcare Access

Regular medical camps, dental checkups, and health awareness sessions for children and their families.

Mobile School

A school on wheels enabling us to reach the most remote settlements and bring education to the doorstep.

Digital Literacy

Introducing computer skills to older children to bridge the digital divide and open up future career opportunities.

Women Empowerment

Supporting mothers through vocational training and self-help groups to ensure sustainable household income.

Help us expand our reach and touch more lives through our programs.
