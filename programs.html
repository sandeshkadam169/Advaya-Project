Our Programs

We implement comprehensive support programs designed to empower children through education, nutrition, and healthcare.

Education Support Program

Provides academic support, school admissions, tuition assistance, and learning materials for migrant and underprivileged children. The program focuses on foundational literacy, numeracy, and holistic child development to prevent school dropouts.

Bridge School Program

Designed for children aged 6-14 who have dropped out or never attended school. The bridge school prepares children academically and emotionally before enrolling them into formal schools through activity-based and joyful learning methods.

Day Care Program

Supports children aged 3-6 years by providing a safe, nurturing environment that promotes early childhood development. This program also enables elder siblings, especially girls, to continue their education without caregiving burdens.

Evening Study Centre

Supports children enrolled in formal schools by offering after-school academic assistance, homework support, concept clarification, and exam preparation to ensure retention and academic success.

Mobile School Program

Brings education directly to children living in slums and makeshift settlements where access to schools is limited. Temporary classrooms are set up within communities to ensure inclusive and accessible learning.

Nutrition Program

Ensures children receive daily nutritious meals and snacks to combat hunger and malnutrition. The program improves attendance, concentration, and overall well-being, recognizing that a well-fed child learns better.

Healthcare & Health Awareness Program

Provides regular medical camps, BMI tracking, hygiene education, menstrual health support, dental and eye check-ups, and follow-up medical care for children and their families.

Community Outreach & Awareness Programs

Engages parents and communities through awareness sessions on education, hygiene, health, and child rights, strengthening long-term community participation and support.
