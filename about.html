Who We Are

Advaya Education & Charitable Trust is dedicated to bridging the gap in education and healthcare for underprivileged communities.

Our History

Providing hope and a pathway to the future

Advaya Educational and Charitable Trust was established on 30th June 2020 with a noble goal to support and empower migrant communities by providing Quality Education, Nutrition, and Healthcare to children from Migrant communities.

Advaya's main aim is to prevent dropouts from education, eradicate child labour and ensure every child has access to education. The families live in informal settlements like slums in Tarpaulin/makeshift houses, near KR Puram, Bangalore.

Vision & Mission

Our guiding principles for a better future.

Our Vision

To provide equitable and quality education support to children from underprivileged, migrant, and marginalized communities through inclusive learning programs.

Our Mission

To set up preschools, daycares centers, Study Centers, Community Centers, and Short-stay homes for the children of migrant children.

Key Objectives

Quality Education

Ensure access for 180 migrant children living in makeshift houses in K.R Puram, preventing school dropouts.

Holistic Development

Provide nutritious meals, medical care, educational materials, and essential hygiene kits for all-round growth.

Community Empowerment

Establish preschools, daycares, and short-stay homes to foster a foundation for a fulfilling future.

Our Reach

Empowering children across diverse regions

Children at Advaya come from migrant families hailing from Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, Odisha, Yadgiri district in North Karnataka, and even Nepal.

These regions face poverty and limited access to education, pushing families to migrate to Bangalore in search of work. At Advaya, we provide the stability and support they need.
