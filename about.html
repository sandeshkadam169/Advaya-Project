Who We Are

Advaya Education & Charitable Trust is dedicated to bridging the gap in education and healthcare for underprivileged communities.

Our History

Providing hope and a pathway to the future

Advaya Educational and Charitable Trust was established on 30th June 2020 with a noble goal to support and empower migrant communities by providing Quality Education, Nutrition, and Healthcare to children from Migrant communities.

Advaya's main aim is to prevent dropouts from education, eradicate child labour and ensure every child has access to education. The families live in informal settlements like slums in Tarpaulin/makeshift houses, near KR Puram, Bangalore.

Vision & Mission

Our guiding principles for a better future.

Our Vision

Empowering communities worldwide to transform the lives of needy children and women from weaker sections of society through grassroots initiatives aligned with the Global Sustainable Development Goals.

Our Mission

Through our Programs, projects, and initiatives, we aim to empower 3 million marginalized communities by 2030, creating a more equitable and responsible society through collaboration and innovation.

Our Goal

To create a socio-economically inclusive local ecosystem where individuals have equal opportunities for development through collaboration and innovative grassroots interventions.

Objective

01

Providing access to quality education, offering scholarships, and financial assistance for needy children in rural and underserved areas.

02

Enhancing educational infrastructure and resources in remote regions.

03

Advocating & implementing child welfare programs for protecting the rights of children, right to education, health, safety, and well-being.

04

Initiatives against hunger and malnutrition for the needy.
