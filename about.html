Who We Are

Advaya Education & Charitable Trust is dedicated to bridging the gap in education and healthcare for underprivileged communities.

Introduction

Advaya Educational and Charitable Trust was established on 30th June 2020 with a noble goal to support and empower migrant communities by providing Quality Education, Nutrition, and Healthcare to children from Migrant communities.

Advaya's main aim is to prevent dropouts from education, eradicate child labour and ensure every child has access to education. The families live in informal settlements like slums in Tarpaulin/makeshift houses, near KR Puram, Bangalore. These places also lack access to proper sanitation and water.

The children here receive all the support they need for their development, such as two meals (Breakfast and Lunch), Snacks, Medical Care, Books, Bags, Uniforms, Sandals, Grocery Kits, and Hygiene Kits. Our Mission is to Bridge the educational gap for children from migrant communities by offering equitable access to quality education, empowering them to overcome barriers, and building a foundation for a successful and fulfilling future.

Vision & Mission

Our guiding principles for a better future.

Our Vision

To provide equitable and quality education support to children from underprivileged, migrant, and marginalized communities through inclusive learning programs.

Our Mission

To set up preschools, daycares centers, Study Centers, Community Centers, and Short-stay homes for the children of migrant children.

Objectives

  • To ensure access to quality education for 180 migrant children living in Tarpaulin/makeshift houses in K.R Puram, Bengaluru preventing them from dropping out.
  • To provide comprehensive support for child development, including nutritious meals, medical care, educational materials, and hygiene kits.
  • To establish educational and community centers, such as preschools, daycares, study centers, and short-stay homes, to increase the level of education among migrant children and empower them for a better future.

Who We Serve

Children at Advaya come from migrant families hailing from Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, Odisha, Yadgiri district in North Karnataka, and even Nepal. These regions face poverty and limited access to education, pushing families to migrate to Bangalore in search of work.
