Reach & Presence

Advaya Educational and Charitable Trust works at the grassroots level to support migrant and underserved communities, primarily in Bengaluru, with a growing impact across multiple regions through migrant families.

Geographical Presence

Primary Location

Bengaluru, Karnataka – KR Puram & nearby settlements

Advaya reaches children living in slums, construction sites, and makeshift tarpaulin settlements, where access to education, nutrition, and healthcare is limited.

Migrant Families From

Uttar Pradesh Bihar West Bengal Odisha Yadgiri (North Karnataka) Nepal
