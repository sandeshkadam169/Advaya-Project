Nutrition Programme

Nourishing bodies, empowering minds, and building healthier futures.

Nourishing Every Child

Malnutrition remains one of the most persistent and deep-rooted challenges faced by children across India, particularly among those from marginalized and migrant communities. Poor nutrition in the early years not only weakens a child physically but also delays learning, affects school participation, and limits future potential.

At Advaya Educational and Charitable Trust, we witness these realities every day. Many of the children who come to us live in makeshift homes, with low immunity, and chronic health issues, with families struggling to afford even two basic meals a day. Hunger dominates their daily lives, and education often takes a back seat.

That's why we started our daily nutrition programme—to ensure that every child at Advaya receives nutritious, home-cooked meals in a safe, loving environment. These meals not only satisfy hunger but also build strength, focus, and joy. A well-fed child learns better, grows healthier, and begins to dream bigger.

Our work goes beyond feeding—it's about restoring dignity, boosting classroom attendance, and helping children break free from the intergenerational cycle of poverty. With every meal served, we move one step closer to a future where every child can learn, grow, and thrive.

The Advaya nutrition program provides well-balanced daily Nutrition, including two meals and two snacks daily, with lunch provided by the Akshay Pathra Mid-day meal program.
