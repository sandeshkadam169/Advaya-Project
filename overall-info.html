About Advaya Trust

Advaya Educational and Charitable Trust is a non-profit organization dedicated to empowering underprivileged communities through education, healthcare, and sustainable development. Established with a vision to create an equitable society, we work tirelessly to bridge the gap for those in need.

Our Reach

Touching thousands of lives across urban slums and rural settlements.

Legal Status

Registered Charitable Trust with 12A and 80G certifications.

Established

Serving the community with dedication since 2020.
