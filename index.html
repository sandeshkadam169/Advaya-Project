Our Footprints

Since 2020, Advaya Educational and Charitable Trust has been working at the grassroots level to support migrant and marginalized children living in slums and temporary settlements in Bengaluru. What began with just 4 children has grown into a sustained movement impacting 538+ children over five years.

Through our education, nutrition, healthcare, and mobile school initiatives, we currently support 121 children daily, provide 100,000+ nutritious meals, enable 300+ school admissions, and distribute 6,000+ education kits. Our healthcare interventions have reached 1,700+ children and parents through medical camps and preventive care.

Our SDGs

Advaya Education and Charitable Trust is tackling social issues through the lens of the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). Our mission focuses on empowering communities and creating a brighter future for all. With every child we educate, every village we rejuvenate, and every life we heal, we're not just changing lives; we're weaving a blend of hope and strength.

Our endeavors, particularly in rural areas, have been exceptional in shattering the shackles of poverty and nurturing the seeds of sustainable growth. Our unwavering commitment to these goals has undoubtedly contributed to our success.
