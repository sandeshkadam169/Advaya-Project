Empowering the Girl Child

Equal opportunities to learn, grow, and build independent futures.

Breaking Cycles of Poverty and Inequality

At Advaya Educational and Charitable Trust, empowering the girl child is a core part of our work with migrant and marginalized communities. Many girls we support come from backgrounds where education is often interrupted due to poverty, migration, early marriage, household responsibilities, and deep-rooted gender inequality.

Advaya works to ensure that girls are not left behind, but are given equal opportunities to learn, grow, and build independent futures. Through bridge education programs, school admissions, and after-school academic support, we help girls continue their education and prevent dropouts.

"By empowering girls through education, health, and emotional support, Advaya is helping them break cycles of poverty and discrimination, enabling them to become confident individuals and future changemakers."

Girls are provided with uniforms, books, school bags, personal care kits, and continuous learning support so they can attend school with confidence and dignity. Beyond academics, we focus on building confidence and leadership among girls through life skills education, spoken English, creative activities, sports, yoga, and self-defence.

Education Support

Bridge programs and academic support to prevent dropouts and ensure continuous learning.

Health & Nutrition

Daily nutritious meals, regular health check-ups, and menstrual hygiene awareness.

Building Confidence

Life skills, sports, self-defence, and leadership training to challenge norms.
